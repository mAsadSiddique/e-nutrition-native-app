Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2025

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Energy Healing (Nutrilife) mobile application, which provides educational content related to food, nutrition, and farming practices.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you register, we collect basic details such as your name and email address to create and manage your account.

Device and usage information. We may collect technical data such as device type, app version, and interaction patterns to understand usage and improve reliability.

Analytics. Analytics tools may be used to measure engagement with our content. Data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

Camera and photo library (profile picture). When you set or update your profile picture, we may request access to your device’s camera and/or photo library. We use the camera only so you can take a new photo for your profile, and the photo library only so you can choose an existing image. Photos are used solely as your profile picture within the app; they may be cropped, uploaded to our servers, and displayed on your account. Providing a profile picture is optional. You can revoke camera or photo library access at any time in your device settings.

2. How we use your information

We use collected information to operate your account, personalize content and recommendations, and improve app performance and features. Profile photos are used only to display your chosen picture in the app.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Limited data may be shared with trusted service providers strictly for analytics, hosting, or technical operations, under confidentiality agreements.

4. Data retention

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Requests will be handled within a reasonable timeframe in accordance with applicable privacy standards.

6. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect your data. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Consent

By using the app, you consent to the collection and use of information as outlined in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or operational requirements.

8. Contact us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at enutrition@myyahoo.com.
